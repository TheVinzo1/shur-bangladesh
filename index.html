    How to listen

    Sounds

    • Higher note = hotter, on the same scale in every division
    • Rain sound = how much it rained
    • Thunder = one of the wettest
    • Bell = one of the hottest
    • Compare mode: left ear = your division, right ear = the other

    Keyboard

    • Space play / pause
    • ← → step one note
    • 1–8 choose a division
    • Shift + 1–8 compare with a division
    • 0 stop comparing
    • T change track
    • S tell the story
    • R read this note aloud

    Using NVDA or JAWS? The keys work while focus is on the player buttons. If they don't, switch to focus mode (NVDA+Space in NVDA, Insert+Z in JAWS).
